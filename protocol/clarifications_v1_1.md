# Prospective clarification v1.1

Date: 5 October 2026. Applies together with study_design_v1.md before the first full v1 experiment. Motivation: independent Claude protocol critique and coordinator mathematical review. No design ranges or primary endpoints have been selected from new results.

1. **Direction versus magnitude.** The sign of repair-versus-no-repair benefit, including downward-closed threshold attainment, is imposed by comparison. M4 is cooperative on the state domain: off-diagonal derivatives are nonnegative, including the one-sided derivatives at xc. The primary calculation measures the size and location of target differences in the arbitrary parameter design, not evidence that repair benefits biology. Do not replace this estimand with an unperformed minimum-dose optimization.
2. **Comparator interpretation.** M0/M2 are monostable mathematical controls, not fitted, time-scale-matched empirical competitors. M3 tau=1,10,50 tests declared input delays, including delays comparable to the observation window. Conclusions do not exclude all slow monostable alternatives. Additional slow cubic or stochastic families are future extensions.
3. **Final-system classification.** Save pretreatment and post-reduction root/stability classifications and whether any attracting root meets each x target. Target infeasibility of settled roots and transient window attainment are distinct. Frozen-d reference roots for evolving M4 are not full-system attractors or a two-dimensional separatrix.
4. **Control timing and cost.** All-control withdrawal stays fixed at t=45 in the amplitude-duration suite. A requested 30-unit pulse therefore has only 15 units of actual delivery under withdrawal. Save requested and actual duration. Integrate cost over [0,120]; [0,30] contributes zero. Save drive and repair cost components separately and their sum. The .08/.4 scaling and equal weights are conventions. The reported frontier is restricted to sampled controls.
5. **Initial-state reference.** Retain original x0-at-t0 sensitivity and report x at treatment onset, exposing baseline loss of initial-state differences. Add a separate prospective nominal onset-state sensitivity with x(30) in {.04,.4,.6,.8}, s(30)=a_pre, d(30)=0, identical four arms, both policies and endpoint/persistence windows. Integrate this suite from t=30; clearly label this different initial-time definition. Exact post-reduction separatrix initialization is a numerical stress case, not a stable patient group.
6. **M4 intervention.** The repair term is `(r+u_r_actual)*(1-d)*x`. The pulse is scaled by surviving structure. Use full evolving-state drift for settling; frozen-d diagnostics alone cannot prove full-system convergence. At the structural threshold the vector field is continuous and Lipschitz, but derivative checks must report their one-sided convention. Target decisions use the declared thresholds; report borderline margins within numerical tolerance separately instead of inventing biological precision.
7. **Measurement scope.** The declared task-only nuisance ends at t=60, before the primary persistence interval. It tests transient measurement ambiguity and cannot explain the primary endpoint. Redundant tasks have known unequal loadings; a shared identical raw shift can be distinguished from latent x when those loadings are truly anchored. A shift proportional to loadings is exactly confounded. Report those cases separately. Instantaneous Jacobian rank is not a replacement for full dynamic/parameter identifiability.
8. **Stage D implementation specification.** Use nominal plus fixed uniform-design indices 0..7 selected before results. The measurement example uses frozen M4 d=.15, epsilon=eta=0 under maintained combination; noise replicates and schedules remain as in v1. Demonstrate constant-coefficient effective-r confounding separately, since a known additive repair pulse can break the simple r(1-d) symmetry. Any extra algebraic examples are labelled explanatory, not additional empirical evidence. Fit recovery/profile/held-out optimization remains optional and must be explicitly deferred if not executed.

The critique is advisory and can be wrong. Its illustrative claim of a low root near .19 at (a,b,r)=(.09,6,.8) was rejected: direct polynomial calculation gives only one physical root, about .84555. The general requirement to report target feasibility is retained. The claim that shared task effects are always indistinguishable was narrowed as above. Reviewer agreement is not validation.
