## Question and estimands

Can a known additional repair pulse distinguish competing latent dynamics, and support prediction of completely unobserved intervention schedules, with the same number of noisy anchored measurements? Report (i) training-selected generator-family recovery and ambiguity; (ii) true-family parameter recovery; (iii) clean and independently noisy held-out prediction error; (iv) optimizer/boundary failures. Selection must use training data alone. Wrong-family selections and failures are outcomes, not exclusions.

## Fixed design

Models M0--M3 use the existing core equations. Initial x(0)=0.6 is fixed and known for every family, trace, fit and generator; M3 starts s(0)=a+0.08. M0 has a,r; M1/M2 have a,b,r; M3 has a,r,tau. The anchored observation is y=1-x plus independent Gaussian noise, SD 0.01 or 0.03; errors are not clipped. The observation map and noise SD are known. No nuisance map or evolving structural model is fitted.

Each generator uses nominal a=0.06,b=4,r=1.05 plus the first two rows (indices 0,1, no selection by results) of uniform 256-row design seed 20261005, ranges a=[0.03,0.09],b=[2,6],r=[0.8,1.3]. M3 tau is respectively 10,1,50 for nominal,row0,row1. Four independent noise replicates per setting/noise level. Dataset RNG uses SeedSequence([20261019,generator_index,case_index,sigma_index,replicate,trace_index]); shared step data and second-trace errors are paired across training designs.

Training A: two independent noisy traces of drive reduction (onset30, no repair pulse, continued reduced drive). Training B: one identical step trace plus combination trace (same onset30, repair +0.4 until45, reduced drive maintained). Both have two traces and 132 scalar observations: times are 2,4,...,120 plus 0.5,1,30.5,31,45,45.5 (66 times/trace). No measured t=0 enters likelihood because the initial value is fixed known. The two interventions are known additive inputs: commanded a=a+0.08 before onset and a afterward; repair is r+specified pulse, not multiplied by candidate coefficients.

Held out, never fitted or used to choose models/starts/bounds: (H1) altered maintained combination onset18,pulse end27,repair amplitude0.25; (H2) combination onset30,pulse end45,full withdrawal45, including restoration of a+0.08. Evaluate each on times 0.5,1,2,4,...,120 plus 18,27,30.5,31,45,45.5 (unique, sorted); independent Gaussian held-out error uses SeedSequence([20261020,generator_index,case_index,sigma_index,replicate,heldout_index]). Whole schedules are held out; no random timepoint split. Both designs share held-out measurements.

## Fitting and selection

Bounds a=[0.005,0.20], b=[0.001,8], r=[0.2,2.5], tau=[0.2,100]. Fit log parameters by bounded scipy least_squares with an analytic forward sensitivity Jacobian, maximum150 function evaluations/start, ftol=xtol=1e-8,gtol=1e-6. Three deterministic starts at componentwise log-bound fractions 0.2,0.5,0.8, none initialized from truth. Pick converged start with smallest training sum squared standardized residuals; store every start and failures. Convergence is SciPy success; report optimality and multi-start loss spread. Failures are never replaced by zero residuals. If all starts fail, that candidate fails and receives no finite BIC. A generator-data case with all candidates failing is an explicit selection failure.

Known-sigma BIC = sum((y-prediction)/sigma)^2 + k*log(n); common Gaussian constants omitted. Select smallest BIC from successful candidates. Report deltaBIC<2 as ambiguity against the runner-up; this threshold is descriptive, not a posterior probability. No held-out error is used for model selection. Report generator-family selection counts with denominators, including ambiguity, paired B-minus-A differences, per-family confusion tables, true-family relative parameter errors, bound hits (within1% of log-range), clean/noisy held-out RMSE for train-selected and every candidate. No p-values or extrapolation to clinical probabilities.

## Secondary frozen-M4 optimized profiles

Hold a=0.06,b=4 known, epsilon=eta=0, true r=1.05,d=0.15 and x0=0.6. Estimate r and frozen d only. Three designs each have132 scalar values: two step traces; step+combination; one step x trace and66 independent direct d measurements. Sigmas0.01,0.03 and4 replicates. Use seed20261021 with design-independent trace noise. At every d in [0,0.8], grid spacing0.025 includingtrue0.15, optimize r in[0.2,4] by bounded scalar minimization, xatol1e-9. Report full optimized loss curves and grid profile sets delta chi-square<=3.841 as an approximate descriptive likelihood threshold, not validated confidence coverage. Fit joint r,d with three deterministic bounded starts for parameter recovery. Constant-input step-only data identify effective repair r(1-d); a fixed known pulse or calibrated direct d channel may break that symmetry. Whether noisy finite data sufficiently constrain d is an observed result. This does not establish identifiability of evolving M4, unknown a,b, unknown inputs or unknown measurement maps.
